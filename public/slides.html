The Real Problem

Giving an AI your credit card is terrifying.

Autonomous agents are now coding, booking servers, and buying tools on their own. But today's payment cards have zero built-in rules for software.

💸

Runaway Loops

An AI stuck in a loop can call expensive cloud APIs thousands of times, racking up thousands of dollars before anyone notices.

🔑

Leaked Card Details

Storing a real company credit card in an AI prompt or env file means one prompt injection attack exposes the entire line of credit.

🚫

Zero Granular Rules

Existing corporate cards are all-or-nothing. You can't say: "Only buy from GitHub, max $20 per purchase, max $100 per week."

The Solution

Meet AgentPay AI: The Safety Shield.

A lightweight proxy that inspects every purchase request before money ever leaves your PayPal account.

🛡️

1. Strict Spending Rules

Set exact limits per agent. If an agent tries to buy something 1 cent above its daily cap, the request is instantly blocked.

💳

2. Single-Use Cards

The AI never sees your real card. AgentPay issues a virtual card locked to that exact merchant and amount, which expires immediately.

🚨

3. Anomaly Detection

AI checks the purchase context. Unapproved stores, price hikes, or duplicate orders trigger an emergency pause for human approval.
